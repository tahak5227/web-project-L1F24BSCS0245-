Your exchange, in order

Five steps. No guesswork.

  1. 01

    Verify your student status

    Register with your UCP email and current student details. Wait for approval before listing or arranging exchanges.

  2. 02

    Agree on the details

    Confirm the exact item, contents, condition and price. For rentals or loans, agree on dates, deposit and return terms in writing.

  3. 03

    Meet on campus

    Choose a busy, permitted campus area during the day. Tell a friend where you are going. Never share passwords or OTPs.

  4. 04

    Inspect, then exchange

    Check pages, test electronics and count accessories together. Pay only after inspection; record the handover and any deposit.

  5. 05

    Close the loop

    For rentals and free loans, return on time in the agreed condition. Confirm receipt and return the refundable deposit as agreed.

Something feels wrong? Pause the exchange. Use Savings & Contact to describe the issue without sending sensitive documents.

Before you pay

Your buyer safety checklist.

  • Check the item matches the photos, model and description.
  • Test electronics, inspect book pages and count every accessory.
  • Meet in a busy, permitted campus space in daylight.
  • Never send an advance payment just to “hold” an item.
  • Keep passwords, OTPs and private student documents to yourself.
  • Save agreed terms and confirm the handover in writing.
