01 / Student registration

Let’s start with you.

Use your own current UCP Lahore student details. All fields are required.

Use your current UCP student email, not a personal address.

Enter the ID shown on your student card.

Use at least 12 characters with a mix of letters, numbers and symbols. Never share your password or OTP.

Student details are for eligibility review, not public listing content. Do not put your student ID or private documents in an item description.
