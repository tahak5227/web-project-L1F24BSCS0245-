## 🔁 UniLoop

**Good Gear. New Owners. Less Waste.**

UniLoop is a proposed student-to-student marketplace exclusively for
current students of the **University of Central Punjab (UCP), Lahore**.
It is designed to help students buy, sell, rent, borrow, and lend
academic essentials and project hardware within their university
community.

## Project Overview

Students often need books, calculators, lab equipment, or electronic
components for courses and projects. Buying every item new can be
expensive, especially when some equipment is only needed temporarily.

UniLoop aims to make these items easier to access by connecting UCP
students who have items to offer with students who need them. It also
encourages reuse and more affordable campus exchanges.

## Main Categories

- **Academic Essentials:** Textbooks, course notes, calculators, lab
    coats, drawing boards, stationery, and study equipment.
- **Project Hardware:** Arduino boards, Raspberry Pi devices, sensors,
    microcontrollers, robotics kits, electronic components, monitors,
    and computer accessories.

## Planned Features

- **UCP student-only registration:** Registration interface requesting
    a UCP student email, student ID/registration number, department,
    degree program, semester, and password.
- **Buy and sell:** Students can list and browse used academic and
    project-related items.
- **Rent and borrow:** Students can offer or request equipment for
    temporary use, with rental duration and return details.
- **Browse listings:** Items can be explored by category, condition,
    price, and transaction type.
- **List an item:** A form for item details, description, photo,
    condition, price or rental fee, and availability.
- **Savings calculator:** An estimate comparing the cost of buying
    new, buying used, or renting.
- **Exchange guide:** Helpful transaction steps, item-inspection tips,
    and example price comparisons.

> **Access restriction concept:** UniLoop is intended for current UCP
> Lahore students only. The registration design communicates this
> restriction. Actual student identity verification and access control
> would require backend implementation and are not provided by a static
> front-end wireframe.
