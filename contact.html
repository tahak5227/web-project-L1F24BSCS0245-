The right details help.

If there is an immediate safety risk

Stop the exchange and move to a safe place. Use official campus security or emergency channels. This form is not an emergency service.

A few things worth knowing.

Is UniLoop an official UCP service?

No. This is an independent student marketplace concept, not an official university service or endorsement.

Are deposits part of the savings?

No. Refundable deposits are separate upfront cash, returned under agreed terms. Savings compare purchase prices with fees only.

Who can post a listing?

Only approved current UCP Lahore students. You can prepare a private draft while your verification is pending.
